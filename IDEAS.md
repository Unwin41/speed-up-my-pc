## Next: Private all-in-one file converter

**The pain:** to convert or shrink a PDF, video, or image, you end up on ad-filled websites that upload your private files to who-knows-where.

**The fix:** one web page that converts and compresses files **entirely in your browser** (using WebAssembly, e.g. ffmpeg.wasm for video).

- Files never leave your device
- Works offline
- No ads, no account, no upload limits
- Drag a file in, pick what you want out, done

**Why people would share it:** "Stop uploading your documents to random converter sites. Use this instead."
